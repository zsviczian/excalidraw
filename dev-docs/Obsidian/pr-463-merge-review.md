## Exactly what upstream changes

- Crop handles now share resize geometry. Shift maintains aspect ratio; Alt crops from the center. Pressing/releasing modifiers during a drag recomputes from the original image snapshot without mutating its crop object.
- Double-clicking a crop handle restores the corresponding image side(s); Alt also restores the opposite side(s). Crop clicks retain editor/selection state, the invisible rotation handle is excluded, and bound arrows follow uncropping.
- Moving an image inside its crop uses an axis-locked per-drag offset. A single uncropped bitmap is drawn faded outside and clipped at full opacity inside the crop, avoiding mismatched pixel alignment.
- Text corner resizing follows whichever axis grows proportionally more. Horizontal corner drags can now grow the font; side-handle wrapping behavior stays separate.
