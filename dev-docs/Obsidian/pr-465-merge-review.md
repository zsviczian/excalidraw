## What changes for users

W binds selected text to an empty shape, wraps free text in a rectangle, unwraps labeled shapes/arrows and restores their original containers on a subsequent toggle, or wraps/unwraps other selections in a frame. Context-menu text/frame actions share the new helpers. Unbound text keeps its rendered wrapping at a fixed width; selection and arrow-editor state follow the freed text/containers. New rectangles use default style and the text's ink, as upstream intends.

Ordinary frames contain edge-aligned shapes without counting their stroke; tolerance handles snapping precision. Established children are clipped consistently with export, including thick strokes extending beyond the frame. A frame around an image can have zero margin. Delete frame leaves children; Cut now deletes the children included in its clipboard payload. The trial CTA changes only excalidraw-app, which is outside the plugin artifact.
